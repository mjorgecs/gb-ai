Traditional no-code moved the barrier from "hire a developer" to "learn a builder"; AI-native tools move it again to "just describe your idea.".
 
The core difference between traditional no-code app builders and modern AI app builders is that they produce exportable code and handle iterations through conversation.

**Fully AI App Builder:** 
This approach is exemplified by platforms such as Lovable, Bolt.new, and Replit, which convert a text prompt directly into a working, and often exportable, codebase that can be iteratively refined through further conversation.

**Strict Ai App Builder:**
This model resembles the approach historically taken by platforms such as Bubble, Adalo, and Glide before the introduction of generative AI, now augmented with an AI layer that automates the assembly step that was previously performed manually by the user.
